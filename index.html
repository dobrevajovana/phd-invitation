Thursday · 14 January
  1. Morning

Matka Canyon

Friday · 15 January
  1. Late morning

    Recovery breakfast

    After the kafana, we have earned a slow one.

  2. Midday

    Matka Canyon

    About 30 minutes out of the city: an emerald lake between steep cliffs and a tiny old monastery by the water. If the weather allows, a boat ride to Vrelo Cave. Lunch by the lake.

    Matka on the map →
  3. Evening

    The old town and the Old Bazaar

    Back in town, a walk past Macedonia Square and across the Stone Bridge, up to Kale Fortress for the city lights, then kebapi in the Čaršija and a drink in the little cobbled streets.

My Skopje

Saturday · 16 January
  1. Morning

    Mother Teresa Memorial House and brunch

    Mother Teresa was born in Skopje, and her small memorial house stands on the spot of the church where she was baptised. A quiet, beautiful visit, then a long, lazy brunch.

  2. Afternoon

    Up Vodno to the Millennium Cross

    The cable car takes us up the mountain for all of Skopje below us. Depends on the weather and wind, so we'll decide on the day.

  3. Evening

    Farewell dinner in Debar Maalo

    Skopje's cosiest neighbourhood, full of little restaurants and wine. One last long night together.

Home together

Sunday · 17 January
  1. Morning

    Farewell breakfast

    Burek with yogurt, the true Skopje breakfast.

  2. Late morning

    Horse ride at the Hipodrom

    A short horse ride at the Skopje Hipodrom, then coffee nearby. It is on the way to the airport, so we can head straight to our flight.

  3. Afternoon

    We fly home together

    Air Serbia at 15:10 via Belgrade, landing in Copenhagen at 19:35. Or Wizz Air at 18:05 to Malmö, landing 20:45, and the Neptun bus at 21:30 back to Copenhagen.
